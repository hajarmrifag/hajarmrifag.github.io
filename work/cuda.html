01 / Scope

Profile first, then keep the winner.

The project implements naïve, shared-memory, and register-tiled kernels, checks them against a CPU reference, and profiles them with Nsight Compute.

Warp-shuffle Softmax was slower than register caching on the tested T4 workload. The measured winner stayed. The unsuccessful experiment remains in the write-up.
